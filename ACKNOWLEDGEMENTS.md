# Acknowledgements / 致谢

Readnote Atlas exists because earlier work made two complementary ideas visible: reading should stay connected to its source, and video can become a navigable learning document.

## Readnote

[Readnote](https://github.com/pheobepotato/readnote) is the original project by the creator of Readnote Atlas. It established the product philosophy used here: preserve the source, add paragraph-level bilingual support, capture exact passages, and store personal knowledge in user-controlled Obsidian and Notion spaces.

Readnote Atlas extends that philosophy from web text to both text and video.

## YouTube Digest

[YouTube Digest](https://github.com/zarazhangrui/youtube-digest), created by **Zara Zhang**, is the open-source project that provided the video-learning foundation and major inspiration for Readnote Atlas. This repository began from its v1.2.0 transcript, translation, overview, timestamp-navigation, explanation, and note workflows. Portions of that code remain in modified form.

Readnote Atlas substantially redesigns the product around a source-first, always-bilingual experience, adds an in-player subtitle layer, streaming and ahead-of-playback translation scheduling, article reading, and a shared Obsidian/Notion knowledge workflow.

Readnote Atlas 的诞生来自两个互补的方向：阅读应该始终与原始语境相连，而视频也可以成为一份可检索、可定位、可沉淀的学习文档。

**Readnote** 是本项目作者此前创建的项目，奠定了原文优先、段落级双语辅助、精确摘录，以及由用户控制的 Obsidian / Notion 知识沉淀方式。Readnote Atlas 将这套理念从网页文本延伸到了文本与视频两种媒介。

**YouTube Digest** 是 Zara Zhang 创建的开源项目，为本项目的视频学习能力提供了重要基础与灵感。本仓库最初基于其 v1.2.0 的 transcript、翻译、概览、时间戳导航、内容解释和笔记工作流展开设计，其中部分代码经过修改后仍保留在本项目中。

在此基础上，Readnote Atlas 围绕“原始内容优先、始终中英双语”重新设计了产品，并加入播放器内字幕、流式与前置翻译调度、文章阅读，以及统一的 Obsidian / Notion 知识库工作流。
